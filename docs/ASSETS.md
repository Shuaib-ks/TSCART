# Assets and sample catalogue

The TSCART wordmark, small SVG mark and fallback illustration were created for this project. They are editable in `public/assets/`. The design does not assert registered trademark status.

The included six products, descriptions, prices and stocks are a **demo catalogue**. Photographs illustrate a style; they are not proof that a supplier sells that exact product. Replace or approve all product photography, specifications and stock before selling. The application does not scrape products from competing stores.

Lifestyle and sample product photographs are from Unsplash. The files are included locally so the storefront does not depend on live image URLs. Reference: https://unsplash.com/license . The collection is used as part of this website, not as a standalone stock-photo product.

See `asset-manifest.json` for each bundled photo's source URL and role. Some photos are lifestyle scenes rather than isolated product photographs.

Typography: **DM Sans** and **DM Serif Display**, sourced from Google Fonts and bundled locally. Both use the SIL Open Font License; the accompanying license texts are in this directory. No external font service is contacted at runtime.
